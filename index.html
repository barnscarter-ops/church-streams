Church Streams

A small tool used by the media team at Full Life Assembly of God, Waxahachie, TX.

Church Streams schedules the church's weekly YouTube livestreams (Sunday School, Sunday Morning Worship and Wednesday Evening Service) on the FullLife Social channel, so each service has its title, description and thumbnail ready before it starts.

It is used only by the church's own media team. It is not a public app.
